            Readings and revisions Selected lineage, earlier revisions and independent readings

            A reading you can question.

            The poem compresses meaning worked out in human–AI dialogue into a sparse seed. Each model reads it, chooses its own forms and leaves a world that can continue. We look for what survives: intention, distinct roles, shared touch, consent and an open next move.

            01 / RECOGNISE

            Recover the relation.

            We read for the poem’s turn, not its vocabulary. A word from the poem counts once it does something in the world.

            02 / TRACE

            Follow meaning into form.

            Every level cites a passage of the poem and exact lines of the world, and says what was carried, transformed, introduced or left open. Expression is one dimension of eight.

            03 / REOPEN

            Leave room for surprise.

            The earliest run is read like the others and can be outdone by another medium or reading. Readings are attributed, provisional and versioned; no winner is declared.

            Why one reader, and why Muse

            What earns each point?

            The reader picks the strongest written level the cited lines support, and says why. A 3 is an ordinal judgment, not three counted features. Where the three sessions split, the score shown is their mean in thirds (3⅓: two at 3, one at 4), and the level named is the one at least two gave. Every level carries a seed passage, exact evidence, a rationale and a confidence; missing evidence stays unassessed, never zero. One reader gave every reading, so differences between profiles come from the worlds, not from different judges; treat close levels as provisional.

            A question can itself be an act: it can challenge a direction, limit an oracle or invite a successor to disagree. We read such choices alongside code.

            Scope and limits

            The retained runs carry their prompt identities and generation conditions. The rubric was written after the first run.

            The readings describe these worlds, one per model and condition, not the models in general. Confidence is the reader’s judgment, not a probability.

            The original is a beatable historical reference. Scores are never added or ranked by polygon area.

            A world can represent cooperation; actual reciprocal response needs its own trial. Previews do not change readings.
